Terrain and Weather

	∙	Difficult terrain from mud, rubble, or overgrowth slowing movement and breaking formations
	∙	River crossings with current strength checks – Strength (Athletics) DC scaling with conditions
	∙	Fog reducing visibility to 10-20ft, disadvantage on Perception and ranged attacks
	∙	Unstable ground in war-damaged areas – Dexterity saves or fall prone

The Unseelie Corruption
scale as the campaign progresses. Early signs are subtle – animals behaving wrong, plants growing wrong, a general wrongness the party feels before they can name it. Later it becomes mechanical:

	∙	Corrupted terrain acting as difficult terrain with a psychic edge – Wisdom saves to avoid minor hallucinations
	∙	Corrupted creatures with extra HP or wrong damage types
	∙	Areas where magic behaves unpredictably – spells have a chance to fail or misfire

Urban and Political Hazards

	∙	Checkpoints requiring Deception or Persuasion – failure draws guards
	∙	Curfews in war zones – being caught out after dark is an automatic encounter
	∙	Informants in crowds – Passive Insight checks to notice someone watching

Structural

	∙	War-damaged buildings with collapse risk – loud noises or heavy movement trigger Dexterity saves
	∙	Sewers and drainage systems like the culvert – darkness, difficult terrain, things nesting inside
	∙	Barricades and fortifications creating choke points that funnel movement

Fatigue

	∙	Long travel without rest accumulating exhaustion levels
	∙	Extended infiltration or high-stress situations requiring Constitution saves to avoid one level of exhaustion
