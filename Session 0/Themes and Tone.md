This campaign does not have clean heroes or clean villains. Every person you meet -- including the ones working against you -- has a reason. You may not agree with those reasons. You may not even understand them for a while. That is intentional.

The world has its own momentum. Things are happening that started before you arrived and will keep moving whether or not you engage with them. Your choices matter -- they shape outcomes, change relationships, close some doors and open others -- but they do not control everything. Some things will happen regardless of what you do. How you respond to those things is where your story lives.

Consequences are real here. Not punitive -- real. Decisions carry weight. People remember what you did. The world does not reset between sessions.

### What to expect

Political complexity without a clear side to take. Information that arrives incomplete and stays that way for a while. NPCs who are neither tools nor obstacles -- people with their own agendas who may help you, hinder you, or both depending on timing. Moments that ask you what your character actually believes.

Combat exists and it matters but it is not always the right answer. Sometimes the most important thing that happens in a session is a conversation.
