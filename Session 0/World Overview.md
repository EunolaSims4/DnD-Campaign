## I. The Continent of Prouvia
Prouvia is the continent on which all matters contained in this document take place. It is a landmass of significant size and varied geography: northern territories characterized by dense forest and mountain range, a central corridor of arable farmland and trade roads, and southern coastal regions whose governance falls outside the scope of this record.

The continent is not unified. It has never been unified. The Crown of Abidor does not pursue unification as a policy and notes that those who have historically attempted it have not fared well. What exists instead is a system of sovereign kingdoms operating in proximity, with trade, treaty, and periodic conflict as the mechanisms of their relationship.

Two kingdoms are of primary relevance to this document. They are treated in full below.

## II. The Kingdom of Abidor
#### Geography and Character

Abidor occupies the central-western portion of the continent. Its borders are defined to the north by the Greywood and to the east by the Alder Road, which serves as both trade corridor and informal boundary with Onara. The kingdom's interior is predominantly farmland and managed forest, with the capital situated on elevated ground at the convergence of three river tributaries.

Abidor is a kingdom of institutions. Its laws are old and its records older. The Crown operates within a system that has outlasted several attempts to simplify it, and the Crown considers this a feature rather than a deficiency.

#### **The Succession**
The Crown does not pass by blood alone. Eligible candidates must be of royal bloodline, but selection from among eligible candidates is made by the people of Abidor through a process of formal declaration.

This means that a claimant with every legal right to the throne may nevertheless never hold it, if the people do not choose them. The Crown has found over several centuries that this produces more stable governance than the alternative. Those who have found it inconvenient are not, by definition, running the kingdom.

The current Crown is legitimate by every measure this system provides. The Office of the Crown Adviser operates in support of that legitimacy and will continue to do so.

#### The Capital

The capital city sits at the northern end of the central trade road, elevated above the river plain, walled on three sides. It is the largest settlement on the continent by population and the seat of all Crown functions. Travelers approaching from the south will see it on the horizon approximately two days before they arrive.

The capital is a working city. It is not a ceremonial one. The Crown's business is conducted here because it is where the kingdom's business is conducted, not for symbolic reasons.

#### Thornhaven

Thornhaven is a market town located at the southern edge of Abidor's territory, at the junction of three secondary roads. It functions as a commercial hub for the surrounding region and as the first and last significant settlement travelers encounter when entering or leaving Abidor from the south.

The Splitroot Inn and Tavern  is the primary lodging and gathering establishment. Its proprietor, Daven, has operated it for many years and is considered a reliable source of local information by those who know how to ask.

The Ventval Woodlands lie to the north and west of Thornhaven. They have been experiencing an irregular condition for several weeks at the time of this document's preparation. The nature of this condition is under assessment.
